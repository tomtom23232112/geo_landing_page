Privacy Policy

We collect what we need to deliver your GEO report and run your subscription — nothing more.

1. Who we are

Everadam LLC ("Everadam," "we," "us") is a U.S. limited liability company registered in Wyoming, USA. We provide automated Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) services. You can reach us at our contact page.

2. What we collect

You give us

  • Domain or website URL — the site you want analyzed.
  • Email address — to deliver your free report and account communication.
  • Name and phone number — collected during checkout for billing and support.
  • Survey answers — your role, company size, and primary goal.
  • Payment details — handled by our payment processor (Stripe). We never see or store your full card number.

We collect automatically

  • Usage data — pages viewed, buttons clicked, anonymized IP, browser type, referrer.
  • Cookies — essential session cookies and (if enabled) analytics cookies.

We generate about your site

  • Public webpage content from your domain to evaluate AI-search readiness.
  • Visibility scores, findings, and recommendations.

3. How we use it

  • Deliver your free GEO Visibility Report.
  • Run the GEO setup and monthly optimization workflow.
  • Send the monthly visibility update.
  • Handle billing, receipts, and support.
  • Improve our product and detect abuse.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it to train third-party AI models.

4. Who we share it with

Only the service providers we need to run the business:

  • Stripe, Inc. — payments
  • Email delivery (e.g., Postmark, Resend, or similar) — transactional emails
  • Cloud hosting — to run our service
  • Analytics — privacy-respecting product analytics

Each provider is bound by a data-processing agreement and only processes data on our instructions.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account & billing data: while your subscription is active, plus 7 years for tax records.
  • Reports & scan data: 24 months after your last activity, then deleted.
  • Marketing emails (if subscribed): until you unsubscribe.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR, CCPA, etc.) you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct or update inaccurate data.
  • Delete your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Export your data in a portable format.
  • Opt out of marketing emails at any time.

Email us at our contact page and we'll handle your request within 30 days.

7. Cookies

We use a small number of cookies: essential session cookies (required), and optional analytics cookies. You can disable non-essential cookies in your browser without losing core functionality.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest. Payments are processed by Stripe, a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified provider. We use the principle of least privilege for internal access.

9. International transfers

Everadam is U.S.-based. If you access our service from outside the U.S., your data is transferred to and processed in the United States under appropriate safeguards (Standard Contractual Clauses where required).

10. Children

Our service is not intended for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be notified by email at least 30 days in advance. The "Effective" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.

12. Contact

Questions or requests? See our contact page.
